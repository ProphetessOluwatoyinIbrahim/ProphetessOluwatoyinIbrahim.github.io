  • Profitability: operating margin improved by about 2.6 points while net profit grew faster than revenue.
  • Liquidity: a current ratio of 1.57 covers short-term obligations, but the quick ratio of 1.14 shows stock is a large part of current assets.
  • Leverage: interest is covered 7.8 times by operating profit, so debt is manageable.
  • Recommendation: review slow-moving stock to release cash before taking on more borrowing.
